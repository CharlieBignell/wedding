FAQ

👔 What's the dress code?

Whilst there is not a specific dress code, we encourage all our guests to dress up for the occasion. Join us in making the day a vibrant celebration with bright and colourful attire - even if it's just your socks! If you're stuck, Fliss would love to chat outfit options 👀.

🎁 Can I bring a gift?

Your presence at our wedding is more than enough and we'd love for you to spend your money on drinks at the bar instead! However, if you'd like to contribute to excursions during our honeymoon in Grenada, we've set up a way for you to easily donate by clicking this link. There will be a card box available on the day.

📸 Can I take photos?

We request that you refrain from taking photos or videos at the ceremony and reception, but feel free during the rest of the day. Don't worry, all guests will get access to the special moments captured by our great photographers.

👯 Can I bring a 'plus one'?

Unfortunately, we are unable to accommodate any additional guests. Please note that all guests are invited to the whole day.
