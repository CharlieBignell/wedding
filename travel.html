Parking

The easiest way to get to Tewinbury is via car and there is plenty of on-site parking. Guests can use the Tythe Barn car park on the day of the wedding, and cars can be left there until the next morning. See the map on this page for more details, and view the full site map here.


Train

If you are coming via train, the venue is only a 5 minute taxi from Welwyn Garden City station or Welwyn North station.
